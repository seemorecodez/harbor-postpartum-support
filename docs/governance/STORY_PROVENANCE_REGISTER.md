## Required approval before public release

Each story must receive:

1. Compensated review by a diverse women-led postpartum lived-experience panel, including women whose experiences differ by race, disability, family structure, birth experience, feeding path, income, and access to care.
2. Language and harm review for shame, coercion, medical minimization, stereotypes, exclusion, and implications that one postpartum experience is universal.
3. Qualified clinical review whenever a revision introduces medical, mental-health, medication, feeding-safety, or crisis language.
4. A named content owner, approved revision identifier, approval dates, next-review date, jurisdiction, and correction path.

No draft may be relabeled as a real woman’s account without her informed, revocable publication consent and a new provenance record. This register does not approve the drafts; it makes their current origin and missing approvals inspectable.

## Community separation

Stories are not the requested anonymous message board. A real multi-user board necessarily crosses the local-only boundary and remains unimplemented pending explicit approval of remote publishing, a separate threat model, human moderation operations, retention/deletion rules, and legal and safety review.
